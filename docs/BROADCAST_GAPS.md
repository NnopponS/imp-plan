# Broadcast comparison: what changed and what is still a fan rule

A rule mentioned by a fan adaptation is a lead, not proof that this app matches the show. Episode metadata came from Netflix; detailed clues below come from unofficial transcript mirrors and are paraphrased. No episode footage, reference-site assets, audio, source code, or rule text is redistributed.

| Game | Episode evidence | What is different in Imp Plan |
|---|---|---|
| Blind Gomoku | [Netflix clip listing, S1E10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4LxJugqygUs); [unofficial episode transcript](https://www.springfieldspringfield.co.uk/view_episode_scripts.php?episode=s01e10&tv-show=the-devils-plan-2023). Top colors stay visible. The side underneath distinguishes ownership, not the top. | The Secret Chamber update adds selectable, engraved covers and keeps ownership hidden until the result. The original cover designs, 15×15 setting, stock behavior and multi-seat league are fan choices; exact original stock and draw procedure are not established. |
| Time Auction | [Unofficial S2E4 transcript](https://www.subtitlecat.com/subs/1738/The.Devil%27s.Plan.S02E04.en.html) describes a concealed hold/release clock, 19 rounds, a ten-minute reserve, and no prize on a tied longest hold. | The default quick game is an eight-round sealed-bid variant with a one-minute reserve. An optional, episode-inspired Hold & release mode uses nineteen rounds and a ten-minute bank. The arming countdown, interruption rules, review windows, and no-prize standings are browser adaptations; this is not verified broadcast mode. |
| Montage | [Unofficial S1E9 transcript](https://www.springfieldspringfield.co.uk/view_episode_scripts.php?episode=s01e09&tv-show=the-devils-plan-2023) establishes five rounds, timed portraits, first correct buzz, cumulative errors and immediate failure on a missed repeat. | Original generated image deck, host-receipt ordering, countdown and slower unscored solo practice are browser house rules. Pieces are fictional, and no broadcast artwork or prize is reproduced. |
| Wall Go | [Unofficial S2E10 transcript](https://www.subtitlecat.com/subs/1742/The.Devil%27s.Plan.S02E10.en.html) indicates a three-seat first heat, orthogonal movement followed by a wall, and a two-seat follow-up. [Reference implementation](https://quoridorgame.com/en/wall-go/rules/) corroborates the 7×7 surface but is not the original. | The fan duel starts in opposite corners, permits staying without an out-and-back path, uses permanent unit walls and ends when either the pawns separate or 40 turns pass. A pawn-exclusive connected region scores by area; shared and empty regions are neutral. Equal totals draw. There is no wall breaking or three-seat heat, and the original setup and tiebreak are not implemented. |
| Memory Poker | [Unofficial S3E2 transcript](https://www.subtitlecat.com/subs/1791/The.Devils.Plan.S03E02.1080p.NF.WEB-DL.Multi.DDP5.1.H.264-1918.html) indicates a duel with 16 study cards, 20 seconds of study, three shared cards, and two marked positions for a five-card hand. An exact tie led to a new deal. | The existing fan mode uses 20 study cards and four rounds of picking five positions, optional one-card help, and more seats. The episode-style two-pick duel is a separate option for two human players in a private room; the original ladder is not implemented. |
| Heavy 13 | [Unofficial S3E2 transcript](https://www.subtitlecat.com/subs/1791/The.Devils.Plan.S03E02.1080p.NF.WEB-DL.Multi.DDP5.1.H.264-1918.html) and [S3E3 transcript](https://www.subtitlecat.com/subs/1788/The.Devil%27s.Plan.S03E03.en.html) describe seven players, fourteen rounds, private numbers and colors, rainbow priority and a penalty threshold of 13. [Replay recap](https://quickinfo.kr/heavy-13-game/) supports no marker change when nobody is penalized. | The fan table supports 2–7 seats, bots, configurable deadlines, deterministic fallback actions, seeded starting marker, and a shared lead when equally ranked unpenalized players remain. The last regular actor is required to place even if someone has deferred, based on the episode examples. No prison or season rewards are reproduced. |
| Piece Puzzle | [Unofficial Korean S3E6 transcript](https://subtitlely.com/subtitle/gwd84ea1tpw9q34l16lnu31n) describes individual stations, eight interlocking pieces of five types, and a check when the player buzzes. | Current game is a cooperative 7×7 silhouette tiling variant. Exact three-dimensional piece geometry and assembly rules need visual review; do not present the current puzzles as the original apparatus. |

The browser review of [Dice Devil](https://quoridorgame.com/en/blind-gomoku) informed the transition from mode and difficulty to an immediately usable board, a persistent turn indicator, and redundant color/mark cues. We do not replicate its illustrations or design. The room keeper, challenger, engravings and atmosphere are original to Imp Plan.
