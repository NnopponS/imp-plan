# Where the games appear

Card badges classify the **show setting or round**, not the accuracy of a game's
playable rules. All playable editions are unofficial fan adaptations. The catalog
continues to distinguish indexed entries from playable ones and preserves the raw
source leads for each entry.

- Main, Prize, Prison and Final day numbers use the per-day leads in
  [the catalog](../src/catalog.json) and the corresponding [rule sheets](rules/).
  Numbered days are competition days, not episodes. [Netflix's Season 2
  explainer](https://www.netflix.com/tudum/articles/the-devils-plan-death-room-release-date-news)
  distinguishes the standard Prison Match from the main game.
- Blind Gomoku is a hidden game inside the Season 1 prison, not a standard Prison
  Match. The [episode 10 transcript mirror](https://www.springfieldspringfield.co.uk/view_episode_scripts.php?episode=s01e10&tv-show=the-devils-plan-2023)
  calls it the Secret Chamber. The Season 2 Knight’s Tour belongs instead to the
  living-quarters hidden stage; see the [episode 5 transcript mirror](https://www.subtitlecat.com/subs/1700/The.Devils.Plan.S02E05.1080p.NF.WEB-DL.x264.DDP5.1-ADWeb.html).
- The four Either Or mini-games belong to Season 3's opening Main Match, not
  the prison. Season 3's Day 1 catalog lead supplies their order; the [episode 2
  transcript mirror](https://www.subtitlecat.com/subs/1787/The.Devil%27s.Plan.S03E02.en.html)
  records Lay the Grass and Memory Poker followed by the first Prison Match,
  Heavy 13.
- Count the Rectangles appears twice in Season 3: the Either Or mini-game and a
  later Exile Zone replay, explicitly called the seventh Exile Match in the
  [episode 12 transcript mirror](https://subtitlely.com/subtitle/b50b8yjzyww4t0yiwuci7v9s).
  The digital boards are distinct fan packs. The [Exile Zone catalog lead](https://www.namu.moe/w/데블스%20플랜:%20데드%20어게인/유배지)
  separately places Divine Test in a hidden Exile stage and Point Gomoku in the
  Exile Zone's final showdown, not the season finale. [Netflix's Exile Zone clip](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Da10EtgGbzY)
  corroborates the existence of the separate setting.
- Color Link and Equation Traffic Light were games two and three of the Season 3
  finale according to the [episode 14 transcript mirror](https://transcripts.foreverdreaming.org/viewtopic.php?p=385991).
  Color Link’s [six-pair fan duel](rules/S3-28.md) is not its timed on-air proof game.
  Equation Traffic Light’s nine-cell show format is not the eight-character fan adaptation. Conversely,
  Season 1's third final slot, Dice Poker, is indexed as planned but not played
  on air; it remains unplayable here.
