## Secret Chamber playable update

Select one of seven engraved covers, or let the game choose automatically. The covered face has a distinct pattern in addition to color; it does not identify the player. Solo opponents echo the last human cover. The match engine reveals the actual sides only after the result. The original illustrated players and chamber are not depictions of any cast member or replica props. The opening scene can be skipped and never starts a match or countdown.
