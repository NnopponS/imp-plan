### Alpha fan rules
Three to sixteen seats, five chips each. Two distinct cards are dealt privately, then each seat discards one. When all lock in or time expires, only kept cards are revealed; non-submissions are passes. The lowest value kept by exactly one seat wins two chips. Eight rounds plus up to three tiebreak rounds. Solo practice fills the minimum with bots. This is not the episode's numbered-deck scoring system; see [episode recap](https://www.thereviewgeek.com/thedevilsplan-s2e2review/) as a secondary lead.
