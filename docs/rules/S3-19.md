# S3-19: Secret Code

Category: Exile  
Seat range in handoff: 1–16  
Broadcast verification: partial  
Fan implementation: playable standalone fan alpha (practice and private-room races).

## Fan rules

Four distinct ordered cards come from three color-and-shape families with ranks 1–4. Each deal has six to twelve clues; a finite solver checks all 11,880 possible arrangements for a unique answer. Select and place cards with tap or keyboard, or drag them with a pointer. A private room gives each player three attempts in three minutes. Each wrong answer moves only that player’s deadline 15 seconds earlier. Unsolved attempts stay private and positional accuracy is withheld until the race ends. First exact answer wins immediately; otherwise the highest number of correct positions across attempts wins after all players finish or run out of time. Equal nonzero scores share the result; a zero-score round has no winner. Offline practice has retries and matching-position counts until the same three-minute deadline. No exile-return or broadcast-equivalence claim.
