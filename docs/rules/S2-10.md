## Broadcast audit

The episode's opening heat has three players, two pawns each, snake placement, and one wall break per player. The survival duel has two players with four pawns each (two fixed, two placed) and no wall breaking. In both, a pawn can take one or two orthogonal steps; returning to the starting cell takes two steps through an open edge, not a free pass. Place a new wall beside the destination. The round ends with territories separated; rank by total controlled cells, then largest individual territory. The transcript gives 90 seconds per turn and a random wall on expiry, but does not specify every case involving no legal pawn move, timeout before selecting a pawn, or a tie after both comparisons. This audit is transcript- and literature-based, not direct footage verification.

## Fan edition rules

- Two pawns begin in opposite corners. On your turn, choose to stay or move up to two orthogonal steps through open edges. The rival’s cell is unavailable, even as a step on a longer route.
- Put one permanent wall on an unused edge beside your selected cell. Choose a destination first, then select a marked edge on the board to apply pawn and wall together. Select the same square again or press Escape to clear; select another marked square to change the destination. Walls can meet at a corner but cannot overlap or cross. A missed turn keeps the pawn in place and the table selects a legal adjacent wall.
- The game ends when the pawns are separated or 40 turns have been completed. A connected region with exactly one pawn gives that player one point per cell, including the pawn’s cell. Shared and empty regions score nothing; tied totals draw.
- Solo practice adds one bot with Easy, Standard and Hard choices. Two-player private rooms can rematch with the opposite starting side. In larger rooms, each pairing plays twice with reversed starting sides, and nonparticipants watch. Native board information is public; unfinished move previews remain local.
- The three-seat broadcast heat, original start cells and wall-breaking rules are not reproduced. This is a fan edition.
