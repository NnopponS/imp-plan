# S1-09: Laying Grass

Category: Main D5  
Seat range in handoff: 2–12  
Broadcast verification: partial  
Fan implementation: playable alpha.

## Fan rules

Nine rotating drafts on a scaled shared board. Draft one connected shape, rotate or reflect it, and extend your territory by edge contact. One redraw per player; passing is legal only without placement. Largest completely filled square wins, then territory size; equal scores share rank. 2–12 native seats with bots. 13–16 seat rotation is not implemented.
