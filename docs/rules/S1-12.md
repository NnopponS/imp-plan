# S1-12: Four-Player Three in a Row

Season 1 · Day 6 Prize Match. Broadcast rules are partially sourced and not verified here. The implemented game uses the handoff's distinct 5 × 5 Fan Mode, not the broadcast stack surface. A [Day 6 recap](https://www.namu.moe/w/%EB%8D%B0%EB%B8%94%EC%8A%A4%20%ED%94%8C%EB%9E%9C%28%EC%8B%9C%EC%A6%8C%201%29/6%EC%9D%BC%EC%B0%A8) and [episode transcript lead](https://www.springfieldspringfield.co.uk/view_episode_scripts.php?episode=s01e10&tv-show=the-devils-plan-2023) are starting points for reviewing the original, not authority for selecting Original mode.

Seat 3–4 at a shared table. One is dealt the private Saboteur role, the rest are Allies. Practice fills to three seats with bots, and the host can explicitly add a fourth. With each turn, mark one open square with A (Laurel) or B (Thorn); each Ally can use two Thorns, while Saboteur has no such limit. Lines count horizontally, vertically, and both diagonals. A three-mark line or exhaustion of twelve turns opens the ballot. A placement deadline passes an empty turn; it never plays an automatic mark for a human.

All players may privately seal one suspect other than themselves so the interface never identifies the Saboteur during play. The Saboteur's vote does not count. A strict majority of all Allies must select the Saboteur; a tie or abstention is not a correct vote. The phase ends when everyone votes or its deadline expires. Allies receive three points each for a Laurel line and two each for a correct identification. Saboteur gets three for stopping a Laurel line, and two for evading identification. Score ties are shared. Neither votes nor spectators' snapshots expose other seats' secrets; roles and points appear at results. The fan ballot is a gameplay adaptation, not a claim about the episode's procedure.

Original-mode audit remains open: episode and exact timing, stack/component behavior, role disclosure, original win/termination and prize exceptions.
