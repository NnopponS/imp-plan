# S1-15 · Hexagon

**Status:** Playable fan adaptation (1–16 human seats; solo supported). Original broadcast parity is not verified.

- The official [Netflix series page](https://www.netflix.com/title/81653386) identifies the episode source. Check S1 episode 12 for exact geometry, timing, and the buzzer/retry rules before offering an original mode.
- The [KAIST puzzle club write-up in DongA Science](https://dongascience.com/en/news/63008) discusses strategies for memorizing numbers in the final. It is not a complete official rulebook.
- Imp Plan displays 19 numbers for 30 seconds, then conceals them. On each target, select three distinct contiguous tiles on one straight axial line and lock one answer in 20 seconds. All matching lines are valid.
- Eight targets in two sets of four. Each correct answer is worth one point; total time across correct answers breaks point ties, then ties are shared. The host generates the boards and validates selections without sending values during the answer phase. This is a memory game, not an anti-cheat system: a player could record publicly displayed values.
