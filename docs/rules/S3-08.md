# S3-08: Auction Bingo

Main Match, Season 3 Day 4. Fan table for 2–16 seats. Practice starts with one bot. Broadcast verification remains partial.

## Play the fan table

1. Everyone gets the same nine-cell card and ten whole coins worth 30 total: three 1s, three 2s, two 3s, one 5 and one 10. Coins are fictional. They cannot be broken or bought.
2. Each of seven rounds starts with a private shape ticket: triangle, circle or star. The tickets open together after every seat seals or the selection clock runs out.
3. On your chosen lot, seal any subset of your remaining coins as one bid, or pass. The room resolves when every seat with a ticket seals a bid or pass, or the bid clock expires. All positive bids are spent, even losing ones. An absent ticket enters no lot; an absent bid is a pass. A ticket still counts toward supply when its owner passes.
4. A lot provides one shape per ticket if anyone bid positively. Its highest bidders split the shapes evenly, discarding any remainder. A lot without a positive bid grants nothing. In each review, the clearing price is shown for sold lots except those tied for the most expensive price across all lots. Neither bidder identity nor the individual bids nor the awarded shapes are published during the match.
5. After the seventh review the dealer publishes shape totals and fits them to each card to maximize complete rows, columns and diagonals, then filled cells. Extra shapes are not placed. Standings rank lines, filled cells and remaining coin value in that order. Exact ties share rank.

Each action phase defaults to 45 seconds; the host can adjust this in the lobby. Reviews last four seconds. A disconnected seat can resume while the timer continues; a sealed ticket or bid is neither reset nor duplicated. Normally only the seat that acted gets its acknowledgment until a public phase change. As in other Imp Plan rooms, the host browser operates as dealer and can inspect its own authoritative state.

## What differs from the show

The Season 3 Episode 9 briefing and the parallel Korean rules summary support the denomination mix, seven rounds, ticket-to-bid sequence, all-pay settlement, split high bids, limited prices, and final auto-placement ranking. The fan table uses an original card layout, faster clocks, explicit timeout/pass handling, a rule for jointly highest clearing prices, and shared exact ranks. It does not reproduce Pieces conversion, automatic relegation or elimination for missed bids, the special guest, or prize mechanics. There are no payments or prizes.
