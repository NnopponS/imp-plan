## Play the fan table

1. Every seat has a closed lower bank containing one black and one white coin. The starter also holds one upper bank. Add a mix of one to five coins to your lower bank, publicly report the additions, and pass it to a rival. Move your previous upper bank down.
2. The receiving seat gets the passed bank on its upper shelf and publicly claims how many of the requested color it contains. The color starts black, then alternates each exchange.
3. Another seat can accept or be first to doubt. On a doubt, open only this bank. A wrong claim transfers one point from the claimant to the caller; a correct claim costs the caller a point. The point loser receives a fresh upper bank and starts the next exchange. If everyone accepts or time runs out, the claimant goes next without opening the bank.
4. All but one seat can trigger a reset. The holdout may privately fill in black and white totals for *all* active banks, then submit them together. All correct earns three points; any error loses two. Other seats see the result, not the guesses. New banks restart at one of each color, beginning with the seat after the holdout. At most four resets.
5. Reach five points or lead after 24 exchanges. Equal leaders share first place. No prizes or eliminations.

Fan timing: party windows are 30 seconds to pass (timeout passes one black coin clockwise), 20 to claim (timeout records no claim), 10 to doubt, 35 to submit a reset challenge, and 3.5 to display a verdict. In solo, coin selection, claims and the reset challenge are untimed; bots still play and may request reset. Bot guesses simulate imperfect memory. No authoritative bank totals are sent to guest views except for a bank publicly opened on a doubt. Public deposits make totals trackable by design, and the browser host must be trusted.

**Not verified:** broadcast timing outside the claim and doubt windows, the precise reset restart procedure, normal finish/tie adjudication beyond the subtitle explanation, and physical props. The synthetic seat range, solo bots, cap, timeouts, whole-challenge entry and all artwork are ours.
