# S1-04: Fragments of Memory

Category: Prize D2  
Seat range in handoff: 1–16  
Broadcast verification: partial  
Fan implementation: playable standalone fan alpha: cooperative room or solo recall.

## Fan rules

Study a generated exhibit for 45 seconds: nine objects, shelf positions, colors, a label and a clock. The scene and accessible text alternative are removed before questioning. Seats rotate in room order. Each seat has 20 seconds to select one objective answer; a wrong or missed answer earns zero. The default is 12 questions, with 8, 16 and 24 available. Casual requires at least five-sixths correct, rounded up; teammates can use room chat. Strict mode disables room chat and ends at the first miss. The team score is shared; no alternate-team or cross-room leaderboard. If more people than questions join, not everyone is guaranteed an answer turn. The study scene was already transmitted to participants, so clients can retain it; this is honor-based play, not anti-cheat.
