# S3-29: Equation Traffic Light / Equation Lights

Category: Final D7  
Seat range in handoff: Duel  
Broadcast verification: partial  
Fan implementation: playable standalone alpha with bot practice and private-room duels.

## Fan rules

Both players choose a true equation of exactly eight ASCII characters. Use digits, +, -, *, / and one =. There are no spaces, parentheses, leading zeroes, or unary signs. The right side is a nonnegative integer. Apply ordinary operator precedence, evaluating multiplication and division left to right; division by zero or a fractional division step is invalid.

Take turns submitting distinct valid equations to solve the rival’s secret. Each position is exact, elsewhere, or absent. Consume exact matches first, then consume each remaining symbol at most once. After a solve, equalize guess counts before deciding the result. If nobody solves after eight guesses each, the highest exact count on any row wins, with fewer guesses to reach that score as a tiebreak. Equal ranks share the result. There is no timer. Only the player sees their secret and their guesses; spectators see lock/guess counts and the outcome. In practice the bot chooses a secret from a bounded corpus and filters guesses against its own feedback. Larger parties use scheduled two-leg tables. No broadcast equivalence is claimed.
