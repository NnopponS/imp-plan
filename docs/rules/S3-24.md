# S3-24: Trial of God / Divine Test

Category: Hidden/exile  
Seat range in handoff: 1–16  
Broadcast verification: partial  
Fan implementation: playable standalone alpha, including private-room races.

## Fan rules

Use each number from 1 to N² once. Every row, column and both diagonals must total N(N²+1)/2. The engraved clue is fixed. The default board is 7×7 (target 175), with 3×3 and 5×5 alternatives and a ten-minute default. Drag or select a tile and then a cell; moving an occupied tile onto another swaps them. Choose the optional clue-only numeral key to solve a unique chain of digit equations. The first valid square wins; an unsolved timeout has no winner. Other players cannot see your grid. No original episode fidelity or season-level rewards/return slots are claimed.
