# S2-08: Triple Dice

**Prison Match · Season 2, Day 4 · 2–8 seats · fan rules**

## Play

1. Your drawer holds four six-sided dice. The first two faces are visible to the whole table, the third is private to you, and the fourth is blind, even to you. The browser host deals all four faces before any picks.
2. Choose exactly three positions. Forecast your **base** points and seal once. Low is 0 to under 4, Mid is 4 to under 7, and High is 7 or more. At 2–4 seats, only Low can occur; at 5–7 seats, Low and Mid are possible; at 8 seats all three are possible.
3. At the end of the round only chosen dice open. Three matching dice beat three consecutive dice, then a pair, then any other set. Within a category, compare matching face, highest straight, pair then kicker, or all three faces from highest to lowest, respectively. There are no wraparound straights.
4. Base points equal occupied seats minus rank. Exact hand ties average the ranks they occupy. A correct forecast adds three points; a miss deducts one, with a zero floor. A missed deadline gives no points that round. For example, two hands sharing first at five seats earn 3.5 base points each. A correct Low forecast makes that 6.5.
5. The open-dice ledger rotates by one seat after each six-second review. After eight rounds, highest total wins; equal final totals share the lead. There are no wagers, purchases, prizes, or transferable points.

The default selection clock is 45 seconds. Practice needs no account or room and starts with one bot. The setup can add bots to fill up to eight seats. A party may mix people and bots. Bots use only their own visible faces and the public dice, not the predealt blind values. Disconnected seats can resume using the normal room reconnect flow; their absence does not stop the selection clock.

The room sends recipients only the shared faces and their own private tray/lock until reveal. Unselected private and blind values stay hidden from the room. The host device holds the full deal and could inspect or alter it; this is not a trustless competition.
