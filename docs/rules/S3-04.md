# S3-04: Lay the Grass

Category: Either Or mini-game  
Seat range in handoff: 2–16  
Broadcast verification: partial  
Fan implementation: playable alpha.

## Fan rules

Four drafts. Players can agree to swap draft positions for up to two earned bonus points, once per participant per round. Each draft tile extends territory; covering one or more marks gives its bonus once and removes those marks. Total territory plus bonus wins, with largest solid square as tiebreak. 2–16 native seats with bots. This is not the complete Either Or event or the broadcast tile overlay rules.
