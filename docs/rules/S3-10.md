# S3-10: Hit, Stay

Season 3, Day 6 Main Match. **Fan table only.** One browser-hosted table seats 2–8 participants; solo practice is one human against one bot. Chips are fictional, cannot be bought or redeemed, and never leave the match.

## Fan table

1. Each seat begins with 20 chips. Each eligible seat puts 2 chips in the pot and receives two private cards. The opening seat rotates clockwise between hands.
2. The limit is always 21. A face card is 10; an Ace is 1, or 11 when that would not bust. Other cards use their printed number.
3. **Hit** takes one private card and passes clockwise to the next seat still drawing. **Stay** locks the hand. Above 21 busts immediately; a seventh card otherwise locks the hand. The clock expiring also stays. Other seats see only the count and status until the hand is over.
4. After every hand is settled, reveal the cards. The highest total that does not exceed 21 takes the pot. Exact ties divide it; leftover chips go to tied seats in clockwise order from the opener. If all bust, every ante is refunded.
5. One standard 52-card deck serves 2–7 seats; 8 seats use two. Keep using the same shoe until a fresh shuffle is required to guarantee every eligible seat could take seven cards, and never shuffle midway through a hand.
6. Deal up to eight hands. An underfunded seat cannot enter the next hand; if at most one seat can enter, the table ends early. With the default 20 chips and eight 2-chip antes, the hand limit is reached before anyone can run out. Highest stack wins; equal leaders share the result. Between hands the host can redeal or wait for automatic redeal after 12 seconds.
