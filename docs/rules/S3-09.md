# S3-09: Hidden Word

- **Venue:** Season 3, Day 5 Main Match (Episode 11).
- **Fan table:** 3–16 occupied seats, people and bots. Offline practice starts with you and two bots.
- **Broadcast status:** Partial. This English browser version is a fan adaptation, not a broadcast mode.

## The fan table

1. **Choose a word.** The host deals five different English nouns privately to each seat. Everyone seals one noun for round 1. The first word is retired in round 2; choose from your original four unused nouns. A randomized setter order serves both rounds. Each seat sets one word in each round and reads every other seat’s clues.
2. **Give a clue.** On your setter turn, the first menu has two broad associations. Stage 2, if reached, offers two narrower clues; stage 3 offers a signature clue. Select one and publish its fixed rating. The original 96-noun English lexicon is authored for offline play: a first-stage association fits six nouns, a second-stage one fits two, and the final association identifies one. Ratings (95–99%) describe these curated relationships, not a live model’s prediction or statistical probabilities.
3. **Seal or pass.** Every other seat writes one exact noun from the word bank or passes. Case and surrounding spaces do not matter. Your own envelope, answers already uncovered this match, and earlier misses for this word can be ruled out. Spend one of two fictional bonus seals per match to add a distinct second guess. You may do this only once for any setter’s word, even across stages. A sealed submission cannot be changed.
4. **Open together.** Answers remain private until everyone seals or the host clock expires. Any number of correct readers earn one point apiece in round 1; a seat scores once, even if it sent two answers. A correct guess ends the word. If nobody is right, the setter earns 1, then 2, then 3 points for each missed stage. The answer is revealed on a solve or after the third stage. These points accumulate and every award doubles in round 2.
5. **Finish.** After both rotations, the highest total leads. Equal scores share a rank, with no extra rounds or consequences outside the match. The word history shows the solved or unguessed words and each setter’s points.

The host can adjust the phase clock (45 seconds by default). An expired choice selects the first unused word; an expired clue posts the first legal clue; unanswered readers pass. These rules also keep play moving if a seat disconnects. A short review opens the guesses before the next stage. Unused seals have no final value.

## Privacy and verification

The browser host is authoritative and necessarily knows the deal. Other peers receive only their own envelope, chosen word, available clue menu when setting, and sealed guesses; shared clues, score and open answers are broadcast at the proper phase. The room does not publish its deal entropy or reveal an opponent’s submission status early. A malicious host or a player inspecting the public association lexicon is outside the trust model; stay with players you trust. No new service or account is required.

The seeded unit fixture covers deal uniqueness at sixteen seats, clue balance, role and timing validation, privacy before opens, bonuses, cumulative and doubled scoring, ties and a complete timed run. Playwright checks cover three independent Chromium contexts over native PeerJS with a loopback-only STUN fixture, 320 px touch and keyboard practice through both rotations, all six lesson steps, and a fresh production offline tab. The native test checks connected ICE and SCTP in all three contexts before verifying sealed answers and their joint reveal. This is not a cross-network play claim.
