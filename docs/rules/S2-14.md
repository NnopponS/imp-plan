# S2-14 · Bagh Chal
**Playable finals adaptation.** A traditional Nepali game. The [Netflix S2 listing and format announcement](https://www.netflix.com/tudum/articles/the-devils-plan-death-room-release-date-news) and the [finals recap](https://www.dexerto.com/tv-movies/the-devils-plan-season-2-winners-eliminations-match-results-3190302/) provide episode context. Here the two roles swap in consecutive legs; the reported broadcast presentation used two boards. No exact broadcast parity is claimed.

Four tigers begin on the corners. Goats move first, placing one of twenty goats until the reserve is exhausted, then sliding. Tigers step or jump over one goat to a vacant intersection two positions along a straight marked line. There are no chain captures. Five captures wins for the tigers; immobilizing the tigers wins for the goats. Goats unable to move after placement lose. The third occurrence of the same position and side, or 100 turns, draws a leg. The result summary aggregates the two legs; larger parties use the existing rotating league.
