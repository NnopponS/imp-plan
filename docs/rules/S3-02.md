# S3-02 · Count the Cubes
Playable fan adaptation for 1–16 human seats. First mini-game in the Season 3 Either Or main match; not a recreation of the whole match.

The [Netflix season listing](https://www.netflix.com/title/81653386) is the broadcast reference. A [season index](https://quoridorgame.com/en/season/3/) corroborates the challenge and its placement, not the exact reward rules.

Imp Plan uses five seeded stacks, 25 seconds per stack, and one point for each exact count. The board is 3 × 3 for the first two rounds and 4 × 4 thereafter. Each base tile can support between zero and four cubes, with no gaps. Three isometric viewpoints and four overhead layer maps make every cube inspectable. The client receives the grid geometry, not a separate answer field. Scores are private until the round ends; ties use elapsed time across correct submissions, with exact time ties shared. The timed solo and party versions are fan designs.
