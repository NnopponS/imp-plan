### Alpha fan rules
One to sixteen seats, simultaneous private paths. Choose a validated 5×5, 6×6 or 8×8 start and a 3–8 minute timer. Open or closed tours are supported (closed only on even boards). Undo is unlimited but does not reset the clock. First finish wins; at timeout compare longest path achieved, then earliest time reaching it. Only current personal route and aggregate visit counts cross the wire. The broadcast challenge used a board sequence; see [format summary](https://dabpop.co/posts/how-the-devils-plan-works) as a secondary lead.
