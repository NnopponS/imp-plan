# S3-22: Flags Around the World

**Exile · 1 human in practice, 2–16 in a private party · fan rules**

## Play

1. The host chooses **Recall** or **Name match** before opening the table.
2. Study 12 distinct flags and their names for 40 seconds. Their numbered stamps offer a non-color memory cue.
3. The board switches to 20 face-up flags: the 12 from the study, plus eight distractors. You have 60 seconds. In Recall, select up to 12 cards you saw. In Name match, pair the listed names to distinct flag cards. You may leave uncertain selections blank.
4. Seal once. Everybody's choices remain private; the table scores when everyone seals or time expires. A hit earns one point and a wrong selection or pair costs one, with a zero floor. Blank places do not cost points; an unsealed entry earns zero. Higher points win, then shorter host-measured submission time. A submitted run ranks above a non-submission at the same score. Identical results share rank.

Every seat gets the same study deck and answer order. Timing comes from the room host; network latency can affect close tiebreaks. Assets and client code are inspectable; players can also take screenshots. The room assumes fair play, not proctoring. Captions and numbered stamps offer an alternate memory route that does not rely on color discrimination.

## Provenance and differences

Broadcast verification remains partial. Two secondary sources describe Episode 12's Exile challenge as memorizing and recognizing flags in a timed multistage format. A full episode and every detail were not independently reviewed. The 12/20 deck, 40/60 second windows, optional name mode and complete scoring above are the fan table, not claims about television mechanics.
