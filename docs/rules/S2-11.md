# S2-11: Doubt and Bet

**Season 2, Day 6 Main Match.** Status: playable fan adaptation, not complete broadcast verification. The web table seats 2–10 people and/or bots. Solo practice adds one bot. All wagers use fictional points or card slots; nothing is purchased, transferred off the table, or paid out.

## Read the table

- Start with 6 points and 5 slots per seat. A shuffled 60-card deck has 10 cards in each of the five named colors and 10 wilds. Every active seat privately receives a card per slot. Wilds count as the named color, but cannot be named.
- The first player chooses a color, a positive count, and at least one point or slot in any combination. A claim means at least that many matching cards across all active hands. The upper bound is 20, or the total number of dealt cards if lower.
- The following player may accept by declaring a strictly higher count, using the same color or switching. The earlier wager is returned completely when an accepted claim is replaced. Or that player can doubt and match the amount, or counter with a higher one.
- Only the claimant and doubter may respond to a counter. Match to reveal, raise within the cap, or fold. A successful count, including exact ties, defends the claim. An unsuccessful count pays the challenger. A fold pays the opponent and never reveals the other hands.
- Each wager is capped at four units and both players' affordable limit; neither player can stake their final slot. A bidder may mix points and slots, but may not withdraw escrow already pledged to that challenge. On settlement the winner takes back their own escrow and collects the loser’s points plus one point per lost slot. Lost slots reduce that seat's next hand, not the hand already dealt. A seat with no spendable points or slots sits out future deals.
- The turn timer starts on entry. A timed-out opener places the minimum, a timed-out responder matches the claim to reveal, and a timed-out player facing a counter folds. The host may deal again after review; otherwise it advances in 12 seconds. After four deals (or fewer than two active seats), score points plus remaining slots; identical final scores share the lead.
