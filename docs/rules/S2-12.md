# S2-12: Equation Pyramid

Category: Prison D6  
Seat range in handoff: 1–16  
Broadcast verification: partial  
Fan implementation: playable standalone fan alpha (offline practice and private-room races).

## Fan rules

Each puzzle deals 15 tiles in a five-row pyramid. Choose any triangle consisting of a parent and its two immediate lower neighbors, and use each tile exactly once in any expression order. Set two arithmetic operators and one of the two parenthesizations. The target is always reachable. Exact fractions are used, so division by zero cannot score.

There are eight puzzles with up to 45 seconds each. One submission per seat per puzzle, with no retry until the next puzzle. The first correct answer accepted by the host earns two points; subsequent correct answers earn one point. Missing or wrong answers earn zero. The round ends when everyone submits or the clock expires. No current-round answers or scores are disclosed until settlement. Ties rank by cumulative time on correct answers; completely tied players share the result. No champion if every score is zero. No claim of verified broadcast timing, geometry, or prison-return scoring.
