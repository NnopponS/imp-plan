# S3-17: Piece Puzzle

Category: Exile
Playable fan adaptation: 1–16 people, offline solo or one shared party board.
Broadcast verification: partial; not a reconstruction of the physical pieces or return mechanism.

## Fan rules

- Complete a 7×7 silhouette using every irregular piece exactly once. A seeded deal chooses one of eight original partitions, transforms the whole silhouette, then shuffles the tray and each piece’s starting rotation. Pieces never flip during play.
- Select a piece, rotate it in 90° steps, then pick the top-left corner of its bounding box. All covered squares must lie inside the silhouette, and must not overlap other pieces or exceed the board. Select a placed piece to move it or return it to the tray. Any exact cover wins, not just the original partition.
- In a party, a placement, return, pass, or clue hands control to the next seat. Offline seats are skipped, and reconnecting seats keep their original order. No bots join this shared puzzle. The four-minute clock keeps running when a participant disconnects.
- An optional clue shows one placement from a known completion, or suggests returning a piece that differs from that completion. The clue does not auto-play, and a second clue is available only after the board changes. Each clue immediately deducts 20 seconds.
- A team that finishes reports its elapsed time plus clue penalties. If time expires first, score the number of target squares covered. There is no individual winner.

## Verification scope

The quantized placement rules, all 16 supported seat counts, host validation, hint clock, timeout and reconnect behavior are unit tested. The browser test solves a deal from visible cells and shapes over the loopback transport with desktop keyboard and a 360px touch client. Cross-network WebRTC was not part of that test. Exact-cover fixtures are finite and are not intended as anti-cheat obfuscation.
