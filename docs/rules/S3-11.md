## How this edition plays

1. Play fourteen rounds on three stands, one red, one green and one blue. In each seven-round hand, each player gets one of each number from 0 through 6, two of each stand color and one rainbow. Stacks and penalties carry over between hands.
2. Lock one number and one color privately. The pairs reveal together. Call rainbows first, then higher numbers within each group; equal numbers are called clockwise from the marker.
3. When called, place on your matching stand. Rainbow can use any stand. The last regular call and every returned call must place; other players may defer once. Deferred calls return in reverse order. This interpretation of the pass corner follows the episode examples.
4. If the new total is at least 13, clear the old stack and take one penalty per old cube, including zeros. Your new cube remains. The last player penalized gets the next marker; if nobody is penalized, it stays where it is.
5. Fewest penalties wins. With equal totals, the player who took their most recent penalty earlier ranks higher. The fan table shares the lead if tied winners have never taken a penalty.

## Browser changes and limits

- Browser presets support 2–7 seats at one table, with bots available in practice and rooms. There is no wrapper for larger parties, prison advancement, or show reward.
- Initial marker is selected from the host seed. A missed selection picks the lowest available number and most plentiful remaining color. A missed placement never passes; a rainbow uses the smallest immediate penalty, then the lowest stand total. These are browser house rules.
- The host keeps incomplete picks and hand snapshots reader-bound; only the acting player sees its private lock. Participants may reason from earlier public reveals. No promise is made that an adversarial host cannot inspect local state.
- Broadcast verification remains partial. No copied game pieces, footage, or artwork are included.
