# S2-09 · Balance Mancala
Playable fan adaptation for 2–8 seats, using a solo bot or the same board shared by the party.

The [Netflix season listing](https://www.netflix.com/title/81653386) is the primary episode reference. The [Day 5 analysis](https://note.com/yoshiki_o/n/n82b392022990?hl=en) is a secondary lead for the last stone's owner and destination point award. Published descriptions of the show use a different balance score and ending. This implementation deliberately uses the supplied fan rules, not claimed broadcast parity.

There are 3N+3 pits for N players. Red, blue and green repeat, with the last two slots replaced by bonus and penalty. In setup, each seat places four owned stones over four circuits, with no more than two per pit. During play, choose a pit containing one of your stones, then distribute its entire stack bottom first around the table. If the last stone lands in a colored pit, its owner scores the final occupancy in that color even when another seat moved the stone. Bonus adds one to each of that owner's three colors. Penalty removes one from each without going negative. Stones are not captured. The match ends after 12 full sowing circuits. Rank by weakest color, then total, and share exact ties.
