## Fan rules

Solo practice uses the same four-minute clock as a room, without other players. Rooms accept 2–16 humans with equal trays and independent boards. Choose vertical, horizontal or diagonal in the lobby, or choose Surprise for a seeded common axis.

Rotate in quarter turns, then place at the selected top-left corner. Pieces cannot overlap, go off the 7 × 7 board or flip. A shape wins only when all pieces are placed, every occupied cell connects edge to edge, and the full occupied set mirrors across the marked line. No single target outline or particular assignment of pieces is required. A solved seat freezes its board while the other seats continue. The room ends when everyone has solved or time ends. A solve beats a partial result, faster solves win, and partial results count tiles only in a connected, mirrored arrangement. The host measures time from match start to receipt of the valid final placement, in milliseconds. Connection latency can affect close finishes. Exact ties share rank.

The host retains the generation witness. Other players receive no private placements or per-seat score before the result. Only the participant's own board and the shared count of completed boards are available in the live projection.
