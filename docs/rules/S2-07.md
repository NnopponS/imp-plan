# S2-07: Treasure Island

Season 2, Day 4 Main Match. This is an episode-inspired fan table, not a verified broadcast replica. It supports one human in solo practice, up to three optional practice bots, or 2–16 occupied seats in a room, including optional bots. The browser hosting the room controls settlement.

## What carries over from the show

The [Netflix series](https://www.netflix.com/title/81653386) is the primary source. An [episode 7 recap](https://www.thereviewgeek.com/thedevilsplan-s2e7review/) describes acquiring arrows in auctions and fitting them together at marked endpoints; [a separate match report](https://www.dexerto.com/tv-movies/the-devils-plan-season-2-winners-eliminations-match-results-3190302/) describes the 9 × 9 obstacle map and ten caches. Recap descriptions differ on the ordering of the televised rounds. This table does not claim exact auction priority, broadcast timing, arrow shapes, clue text, point allocation, or the original map. All images and wording here are original.

## Digital fan rules

1. Begin with 15 fictional chips and a one-step arrow. Each seat contributes two arrows to the market size; the market reveals those lots one at a time before exploration. Seal an integer bid from 1 up to your current balance or pass. Only the winner pays, and an all-pass lot goes unclaimed. Break ties by reading the seating order from the priority seat shown for that lot. Priority advances one seat with each lot.
2. On the original chart, start at E5 and rotate each owned arrow in 90° increments. Append each new arrow to the tip of the prior one. Stay on land, never revisit a square, and use each physical arrow only once per route. All arrows return to your cabinet at the start of each expedition. Loans and trades are not part of this fan table.
3. Privately seal one route or pass each expedition. The host settles once all seats have sealed or the phase clock ends. Silence is a pass. Only the last square of a route is dug. The map has ten hidden chests, four of them reliable clue caches one step from E5. The other six are on land two to four grid steps from E5, and one holds the treasure. A clue gives the minimum number of grid steps ignoring obstacles and the parity of the treasure's row number. Rows are numbered 1 through 9.
4. Any finder earns ten bonus points. All players who find it in the same expedition share first. If no one finds it in five expeditions, compare the number of distinct clue caches found, then the closest attempted dig to the treasure. No dig ranks below any dig for the distance tiebreak. Exact ties share rank. The treasure location becomes public at the end.

The route editor, live chart, read-only preview and worked tutorial use the same chart geometry. In the match, current bids, routes and notebooks are projected only to their own seats; all players see seal counts and the previous lot's winner and price. Bots use their own view and public board geometry, never the hidden chests or the host's random seed.
