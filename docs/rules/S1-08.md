# S1-08: Scale Game

Prize D4 · 1–16 humans · playable fan adaptation · broadcast rules unverified

Five labeled minerals have different integer weights between 1 and 20. The host reveals Brass's weight, its rank among the five from lightest to heaviest, and a menu of four to seven candidate weights for the other minerals. No hidden measurements are sent. Each pan holds 1–6 pieces, with at most four copies of any one mineral per pan. Identical loads are not a useful measurement and are rejected. The result is only left lighter, balanced, or left heavier.

The current operator chooses one weighing and passes the turn clockwise, or locks the final mineral-by-mineral answer. Everyone shares the same weighing history and the room can discuss. The answer must use the published menu and assign distinct weights; a valid answer ends play, scoring one per correctly labeled mineral including Brass. A clean five is a pass. Twelve readings exhaust the scale but still allow the final answer until the five-minute deadline. If time runs out, the result says no answer was locked. The solution is shown after play. Solo practice has the same timer and rules, without other operators or bots.

## Why each deal is solvable

Every menu value has a whole-piece balance against Brass using one to four pieces on each side. Test the middle candidate for an unknown mineral: equality solves it; a lighter or heavier result leaves at most three possibilities. The next middle test solves it or leaves a singleton. Four unknowns therefore need at most eight legal readings (even without using the published rank or eliminating distinct weights), within the limit of twelve. The curated menu makes this a bounded fan adaptation, not an arbitrary draw from all integers in the range.
