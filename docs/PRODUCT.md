# Product

Imp Plan is an English-first, unofficial fan arcade for private strategy game nights. Charcoal, gold and ivory set the tone for the lobby and game tables. Online parties require a nickname. Friends can join with a six-character invite code or a copied link. Local practice starts without a nickname, and there are no accounts.

The alpha candidate covers 28 playable fan adaptations out of 64 indexed challenges across Seasons 1–3: 9 of 18 in Season 1, 7 of 17 in Season 2 and 12 of 29 in Season 3. The index also includes unplayable entries. No original broadcast mode is verified.

Online parties need the host's browser to stay open and a direct peer connection. Some networks cannot connect; loopback tests do not establish that an invite works across separate internet networks. Offline practice is available once the page loads.
