**Goal:** Play the S1 territory draft and the S3 Either Or tile challenge as distinct fan modes.
**Architecture:** Share placement and seeded drafting logic in grass.ts, use the host for authoritative actions, and use one touch/keyboard board with separate rules and scoring.
**Stack:** TypeScript, React, Vitest, Playwright, Blender.
**Spec:** docs/rules/S1-09.md and docs/rules/S3-04.md. These are fan modes, not a claim of broadcast accuracy.
