# Imp Plan: The Sixteen Seats

A silent 30-second, first-visit opening. An engraved book opens onto four tables of four original players. In turn we see a Morris mill, a time auction, an escape through a fence, and a completed color route. Each group earns a different seal; the last scene brings the four seals and all sixteen players back together. The chambers and player portraits are original site artwork, framed with new SVG sets, props, and animated limbs. No footage, audio, network service or animation library.

1. Browser tests: real 30-second timer via the Playwright clock, all four moves and 16 unique characters, end, public controls, reload/new tab, reduced motion, direct invites, offline, and phone skip.
2. Stage: separate timing and responsive SVG from the entry lifecycle. Draw original table/prop geometry; animate each action and caption the story visually; group four enters per act. Keep progress and fixed, instantly accessible Skip/Escape. Existing links stay usable during intro.
3. Navigation: lengthen the independent, nonblocking page-turn cue without lengthening game input or page navigation.
4. Verify sampled real frames at desktop, mobile portrait, and mobile landscape, plus normal build/CI and published page.
