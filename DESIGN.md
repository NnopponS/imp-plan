# Imp Plan interface
The product is a playable board-game library, not a concept presentation. Show the move, the board, or a useful decision before explaining the brand. The landing page offers a real warm-up from a shipped game and obvious solo or room entry. Season views primarily browse games; they do not repeat the landing hero.

Use charcoal for the arena, ivory for boards and rule sheets, antique gold for key actions and scored feedback, and muted crimson for warnings or an opposing side. Work in strokes and crisp folds inspired by a printed rule sheet. No ornamental 3D, giant decorative headings, fake metrics, generic reticles, or infinite animation. A game-specific asset may show pieces, cards, cubes, or a position that explains its mechanic.

References: [Dice Devil](https://quoridorgame.com/en/) for game-first discovery, [Lichess](https://lichess.org/) for short paths from difficulty selection to play, and [Board Game Arena](https://en.boardgamearena.com/) for board-first session hierarchy. None of their art, copy, code or styling is reused. This is a separate fan project.

Review order (adapted from [Impeccable](https://github.com/pbakaus/impeccable)): distill decorative content, clarify the primary action, adapt for narrow and touch layouts, and audit interaction, contrast, motion, focus and keyboard use. Check 320/390 px mobile and desktop at actual viewport sizes. Keep a full-width CTA reachable without relying on hover or motion.

Distinguish Main Match, Prison, Exile and Final Match by game context and artwork, not copied logos or decorative fragments. The specification calls for original and purpose-built art for all 64 entries, timed/turn feedback, interactive tutorials, sound and reduced motion, and evidence across phone, tablet and desktop. Work through the catalog in verified increments; do not show a mock entry as playable or claim the full design target is met before then.
